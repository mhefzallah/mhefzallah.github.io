Context

A multi-branch Saudi retail group ran its stores, accounting, HR and website on Odoo 17 with 63 custom modules. Staying on 17 meant falling behind on support and new features; upgrading meant every customisation could break.

What I built

  1. 01

    Audit

    Mapped all 63 modules, their dependencies and every v19 breaking change that touched them.

  2. 02

    Rewrite

    Updated ORM calls, XML views and OWL components module by module.

  3. 03

    Critical flows

    Multi-branch POS with split payments and credit limits, ZATCA Phase 1 e-invoicing, Telr payments, inventory reports, HR and website.

  4. 04

    Validate & release

    Tested on Odoo.sh staging with real data, then released to production.

  5. 05

    New modules on v19

    Telr payment gateway, purchase approval flow, stock demand lock and a demand-based re-order report.

Key decisions

  • Migrate module by module on staging rather than big-bang, so each fix could be verified against real data.
  • Keep payments and e-invoicing untouched until last-mile testing — the flows the business can’t afford to lose.
