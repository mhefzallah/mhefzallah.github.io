Context

A secondary-school biology teacher was running lectures, exams, attendance and payments across WhatsApp groups and spreadsheets. Parents had no view of attendance or grades.

What I built

  1. 01

    Teacher dashboard

    Students, active subscriptions, income, exams and today’s attendance rate, plus pending payment requests.

  2. 02

    Course content

    Chapters and lessons with video, mind maps, quizzes and free preview lessons.

  3. 03

    Parent portal

    Attendance calendar, exam grades, alerts for absences and one-tap WhatsApp to the teacher.

  4. 04

    Subscriptions & payments

    Subscription plans and payment approval, with notifications to students.

  5. 05

    Mobile apps

    The same React codebase shipped to Android and iOS with Capacitor, and installable as a PWA.

Key decisions

  • Supabase for auth, database and storage — a full backend without running servers for a single-teacher business.
  • Capacitor instead of a separate native app, so web and mobile ship from one codebase.
