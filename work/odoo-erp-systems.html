Context

Most of my work lives inside clients’ ERPs, so it can’t be shown on screen. These are the systems behind it — what each one does and what changed for the business.

What I built

  1. 01

    Saudi HR & payroll — 40+ modules

    End-of-service benefits, Iqama tracking, air tickets, attendance and payroll for full Labor Law compliance, rolled out to several enterprise clients on Odoo 18 and 19.

  2. 02

    Delivery operations — 500+ shipments a day

    Agent assignment engine, live status tracking and SLA reporting; SMSA Express and OneStock labels generated automatically, saving 3+ hours a day; hourly database backups to Google Drive.

  3. 03

    Automotive dealership

    Pre-delivery inspection (PDI), port receiving inspection (VRI), VOR requests, vehicle reservations and B2B dealer credit limits with mandatory document checks.

  4. 04

    Government ERP — 1,000+ users

    Maintenance and upgrades for an Odoo 11 Enterprise system serving 10+ departments: 12+ upgrades and data migrations, 50+ users trained, no unplanned downtime.

  5. 05

    Construction, retail & manufacturing

    Job costing, asset management and subcontractor billing with a PHP/MySQL → Odoo migration; multi-warehouse POS with Neoleap terminals, return pickings and serial tracking; approval flows and advance/retention invoicing.

  6. 06

    Mobile & analytics

    REST APIs with JWT for a mobile app and license activation; revenue projections, transaction dashboards, probation-assessment automation and org-chart export to PowerPoint.

Key decisions

  • Build on standard Odoo objects wherever possible, so clients can keep upgrading.
  • Staging-first delivery on Odoo.sh with weekly sprints, code review and user acceptance testing for every client.
