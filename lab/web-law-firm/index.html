Practice areas

Where we are retained

Each matter is led by a partner and staffed by a small, steady team — the same people from first call to final signature.

  1. I

    Corporate & Commercial

    Company formation, shareholder agreements, joint ventures and commercial contracts under the Saudi Companies Law.

  2. II

    Dispute Resolution

    Litigation before commercial courts and arbitration under SCCA and ICC rules, from pre-action strategy to enforcement.

  3. III

    Real Estate & Construction

    Off-plan sales, development agreements, FIDIC contracts and construction claims.

  4. IV

    Employment

    Labour Law compliance, Saudisation strategy, executive contracts and workplace investigations.

  5. V

    Banking & Finance

    Facility agreements, Islamic finance structures, security packages and restructurings.

  6. VI

    Intellectual Property

    Trademark portfolios, licensing, technology transfer and enforcement before SAIP.

  7. VII

    Family Business & Wealth

    Family charters, succession planning, waqf structures and estate administration.

  8. VIII

    Regulatory & Compliance

    Licensing, data protection (PDPL), anti-corruption and regulator engagement.

Our approach

Three commitments we put in every engagement letter

  1. I

    Plain answers

    Advice written for the decision-maker: what the law says, what it means for you and what we recommend — on one page.

  2. II

    Fixed scopes

    A clear scope and fee estimate before work begins, with no surprises on the invoice.

  3. III

    Partner-led

    A named partner is accountable for every matter and reachable directly, in Arabic or English.
